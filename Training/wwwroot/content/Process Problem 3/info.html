Customer states that this machine never outlets a board unless the operator reaches in and moves the board off the PIP Sensor.

Hint 1

All Lane Entry Conditions are correct.

Hint 2

No scripts are missing or misconfigured.

Hint 3

A Lane has been moved.

Answer

Two lanes have been swapped.

The `Move Out Of Machine` Lane and the `Board Available` lane have been swapped.

Drag the lanes to re-order them into the correct orientation.
